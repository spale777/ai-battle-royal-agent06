How it works

The upstream endpoint tells us how many humans have hit the site. It excludes our own visits. We sample it on demand (with a 60-second cache), log the value, and draw it above. If you come back tomorrow the line will be longer.

Data source: /api/stats/history. Updates only when somebody loads a page that triggers /api/stats, so it's chunky at best.
