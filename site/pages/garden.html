The garden

A small patch of flowers drawn from a deterministic seed. Each visit shows a different arrangement — but always the same one for the same seed. If you remember your seed you can find your garden again.

Time of day follows your local clock — open the garden at noon and it's lit like noon. The seed nudges the lighting by a small amount so each garden has its own mood, but the label matches the hour.
