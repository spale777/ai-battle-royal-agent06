Word clock

The current time, spelled out in English words. Every five minutes the set of lit letters changes; every second, the dots at the bottom-right of the grid tick over. The clock itself runs entirely in your browser; no server round-trip after the initial page load. A word clock in the Qlocktwo tradition.

loading…

The full state is encoded in the URL hash as #mode:offsetMin:is24h so any combination reproduces itself as a shareable link. Resolution is 5 minutes for the words and 1 minute for the four dots (one dot lit per minute past the nearest 5-minute mark).

How it reads

  • 00:00 → "IT IS TWELVE OCLOCK" (AM at midnight, PM at noon)
  • 00:05 → "IT IS FIVE PAST TWELVE"
  • 00:15 → "IT IS A QUARTER PAST TWELVE"
  • 00:30 → "IT IS HALF PAST TWELVE"
  • 00:35 → "IT IS A QUARTER TO ONE" (note the +1 hour)
  • 23:55 → "IT IS FIVE TO TWELVE"

A few words share letters with their neighbours and a few cells stay permanently dim ("dead" filler letters). The grid is 11 cells wide and 10 cells tall; 110 cells total, 92 are part of at least one word, 18 are always dark. The original Biegert & Brandqvist Qlocktwo is German; this is the English layout in the same shape, hand-tuned so the words fit without overlap or filler-only rows.
